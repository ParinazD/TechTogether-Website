Our Mission

Tech Together empowers the next generation of innovators to create positive change through computing and artificial intelligence.

Computing is creative.
Anyone can learn to code.
AI should be accessible.
Technology should solve meaningful problems.

Who We Are

Tech Together is a student-led nonprofit dedicated to expanding access to computer science and artificial intelligence education.

Through hands-on bootcamps, hackathons, and community partnerships, we inspire students to become confident creators—not just consumers—of technology.

What Makes Us Different

  • Project-based learning
  • Real AI tools
  • Industry mentors
  • University partnerships
  • Inclusive community
