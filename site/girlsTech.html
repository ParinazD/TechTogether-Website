Learn. Build. Change the World.

Tech Together's Girls Tech program teaches girls coding, entrepreneurship, and teamwork while they create an app solving a real-world problem. At the end of the program, the girls will submit their projects to the international Technovation Challenge.

Join Our Program   Learn More

Program Information

👩 Who Can Join?

Open to girls ages 8–15. No prior coding experience is required!

🗓 Program Schedule

Weekly meetings from September '26 – April '27

Meeting Times: TBD

💻 What You'll Need

Students should have access to a laptop and stable internet connection.

What You'll Learn

Coding

Learn HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Thunkable, and MIT App Inventor and app development through beginner-friendly projects. Girls will ultimately choose one of these platforms to build their final app.

Critical Thinking

Work with teammates to identify real community challenges, brainstorm creative solutions, and build user-friendly apps.

Leadership / Entrepreneurship

Develop presentation skills, collaborate with mentors, and confidently share your project with judges at Technovation.

Your Journey

Weeks 1–2

Brainstorm

Research community problems and design an innovative app that makes a positive impact.

Weeks 3–4

Learn to Code

Explore programming basics through fun lessons and hands-on coding activities.

Weeks 5–7

Build Your App

Turn your ideas into a working application while receiving feedback from mentors.

Final Week

Submit & Celebrate

Present your finished project and submit it to the international Technovation Challenge.

Why Join?

💜 Build Confidence

Gain technical skills while learning in a supportive environment with mentors and teammates.

🌎 Make an Impact

Design technology that solves meaningful community problems and creates positive change.

🏆 Compete Globally

Showcase your work by participating in the international Technovation Challenge alongside students from around the world.

Ready to Start?

Whether you've never written a line of code or already love programming, Tech Together welcomes girls who are excited to learn, create, and make an impact through technology.
