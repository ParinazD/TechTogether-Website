Who We Are

Tech Together Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Our mission is to empower the next generation of innovators to create positive change through computing and artificial intelligence.

Inspired by a "think globally, act locally" call to action at the UN's Commission on the Status of Women, we sought to create a program to address the gender gap within our computer science community.

Tech Together was founded by Parinaz Dastur and Talia Ye with the goal of sharing our passion for computing with younger girls and inspiring them to pursue their interests in computing. We strive to provide a strong foundation in computer science by introducing computing fundamentals, engaging them with fun and interactive activities, and walking students through programming challenges.

We coached students online to compete in the American Computer Science League (ACSL), co-hosted in-person meetings, and taught Girl Scouts the computing elements needed to fulfill their Coding For Good badges.
